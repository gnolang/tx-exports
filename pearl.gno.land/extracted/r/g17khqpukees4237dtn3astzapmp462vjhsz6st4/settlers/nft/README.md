# Gno.land Settlers

Settlers are small pixel-art gnomes, and each one belongs to a single Gno.land account, forever. A Settler
can't be sold, traded, or given to anyone else. Once it's yours, it stays yours.

## Why they exist

Settlers are a thank-you to the people who were part of Gno.land from the very beginning. Anyone whose
account already held at least 15 GNOT when the `gnoland-1` chain launched can claim one Settler. It's a
simple, permanent way of saying "this account was here at the start."

## No two are alike

Nobody picks how their Settler looks. Its skin tone, hat, beard, clothes, glasses and background are all
worked out automatically from the account that claims it and the moment it's claimed, and the collection
makes sure the same combination is never drawn twice. So every Settler is one of a kind, and it's a little
surprise to see which one you get.

## One per account

Each eligible account can claim exactly one Settler, on a first-come, first-served basis, until all of them
are gone (there are 3,000 in total). There's no cost beyond the usual cost of sending a transaction.

## How to get one

If your account was eligible, you can claim your Settler from the mint page at
[jeronimoalbi.github.io/gnoland-settlers](https://jeronimoalbi.github.io/gnoland-settlers/). Once claimed, you
can look up any Settler, and see who it belongs to, right here in this realm.

## Minting without the web page

If you'd rather not use the mint page at all, or just want to see everything for yourself first, you can do
the whole thing by hand with a terminal. Nothing here needs trusting a website: the list of eligible accounts
and your proof are both worked out from the public genesis of `gnoland-1`, and you can check every step
following the instructions you can find in [jeronimoalbi/gnoland-settlers](https://github.com/jeronimoalbi/gnoland-settlers)
repository.
